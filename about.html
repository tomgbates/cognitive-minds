How Suegné teaches

Not simply showing which formula to use

Suegné works through the science with learners, helping them understand why a method works before expecting them to reproduce it.

That means analysing questions, discussing why one approach is appropriate, and practising enough variation that learners are not dependent on a single memorised pattern.

The aim is greater independence: a learner who can look at an unfamiliar question, decide what it is asking and apply what they know with confidence.

Understand

Make the concept and method clear enough that the learner can explain what they are doing.

Think

Build the reasoning needed to choose an approach instead of relying on memorised steps.

Apply

Use that understanding in exercises, tests and unfamiliar exam-style questions.
