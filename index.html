Science can feel difficult for very different reasons

Some learners need help rebuilding the basics. Others understand the content but struggle to apply it when a question looks unfamiliar.

01

“They understand it in class, but can't do it alone.”

Knowing the content is different from knowing how to work through a problem independently.

02

“They know the formula, but don't know when to use it.”

Memorising a method only helps when the next question looks exactly like the last one.

03

“One unfamiliar question completely throws them.”

Learners need practice identifying what is being asked and deciding how to approach it.

04

“They work hard, but still don't feel confident in science.”

Understanding why a method works can make the subject feel far less intimidating.

Meet Suegné

A love for science, learning and helping it make sense

Suegné Bates has been tutoring for more than five years, working with high school learners, homeschool students and university students.

With a background in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Psychology and Criminology, she has always had a love for understanding how things work.

Her aim is not simply to show a learner which formula to use. She works through the science with them, explains why the methods work and helps them build the confidence to approach questions for themselves.
