The Cognitive Minds Approach

How learning becomes confidence

Strong science results come from more than remembering formulas. Learners build confidence when they understand what they are doing, can reason through a problem, and know how to apply what they have learned.

Who It's For

Tutoring that meets each learner where they are

Every learner struggles with something different. Some need help rebuilding the basics, some understand the work but struggle to apply it, and others want to push themselves further.

Suegné works through real Physics and Chemistry with each learner, adapting her teaching to help them understand the science, work through problems confidently and reach their own academic potential.

01

Making the science make sense

For learners who feel lost in certain topics or have gaps in their understanding. Suegné works through the actual Physics and Chemistry with them, breaking difficult ideas down and explaining them in different ways until they start to make sense.

02

Turning understanding into results

For learners who know some of the work but struggle when the question looks different from what they have practised. They work through problems together, learning how to understand what is being asked, choose an approach and apply what they know.

03

Building confidence and independence

The goal is not only to help a learner through the next test. Suegné wants each student to become more confident in their own ability to think through a problem, understand why a method works and tackle unfamiliar questions without relying only on memorised steps.

Meet Suegné

A love for science, learning and helping it make sense

Suegné Bates has been tutoring for more than five years, working with high school learners, homeschool students and university students.

With a background in Chemistry, Biochemistry, Psychology and Criminology, she has always had a love for understanding how things work. That same curiosity shapes the way she teaches Physics and Chemistry.

Her aim is not simply to show a learner which formula to use. She works through the science with them, explains why the methods work and helps them build the confidence to approach questions for themselves.
