What happens in class

Understanding first. Then practice. Then application.

The goal is not to memorise a route through one familiar question. Learners practise recognising what a question is asking and choosing an approach they understand.

Understand

Break down concepts, identify gaps and make sure the method itself makes sense.

Work it through

Use real Physics and Chemistry problems to build reasoning rather than pattern-matching.

Apply

Practise unfamiliar questions so learners can analyse what is being asked and respond with confidence.
